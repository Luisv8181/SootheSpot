# Quiet Rain artwork provenance

Original SootheSpot artwork generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-11. No personal information was submitted. The fictional setting is not a photograph of a real destination. No third-party imagery or external asset license is used. Visually reviewed both generated outputs and rendered desktop/phone crops. Sharp converted PNG to WebP at quality 87, without stretching or adding drops. Runtime droplets and clearing are authored code, separate from the image.

- `public/worlds/quiet-rain.webp`: 1536 × 1024, 223,390 bytes. Landscape composition.
- `public/worlds/quiet-rain-portrait.webp`: 1024 × 1536, 238,904 bytes. Portrait adaptation; selected through a local picture source for narrow screens.

## Landscape prompt

Create an original premium cinematic environment photograph for a calming interactive website world called Quiet Rain. No text, no UI. Landscape wide composition, 1536x1024 or similar. The viewer is sheltered inside a quiet minimalist timber retreat at blue hour, looking out through ONE huge uninterrupted clear glass window onto a misty emerald mountain valley with a small lake, layers of forest and soft clouds. Subtle warm amber light from a small beautiful paper lamp at the far lower right interior edge, a dark wooden sill across very bottom, a tiny fern at far lower left. Mostly unobstructed view: mountain valley and water should fill 85% of image. No window mullions or frames across center. Photographic realism, exquisite atmospheric depth, wet blue-green dusk, delicate warm reflections on glass near edges, dark navy upper corners and bottom suitable for readable white controls. Rainy weather but DO NOT paint water droplets or fog on the glass; those will be an interactive code layer. No person, no city, no lightning, no dramatic storm, no bright sun, no stars, no logos. Compose a focal distant valley light near center-left, quiet shelter feeling. Rich nuanced colors, high-end architectural photography, tactile wood, soft natural light, elegant and soothing rather than fantasy. This is artwork to ship locally with an app; do not include any interface.

## Portrait prompt

Landscape generated output provided as the edit reference. Recompose this environment photograph as a beautiful portrait 2:3 mobile background, preserving the same photorealistic misty emerald mountain lake at blue hour, same distant warm valley lights and soft cloud layers. The viewer is sheltered inside the same timber retreat. Keep a small warm amber paper lamp clearly visible in the LOWER RIGHT CORNER (fully within portrait frame), tiny fern lower left, wooden sill across bottom; they should occupy less than 15% of composition. Uninterrupted clear glass panorama: lake and layered mountain valley center, no window mullions crossing the picture. Maintain cool blue-green exterior contrasting amber reflected light near bottom edges. Keep sky overcast dusk, not dramatic, no lightning. Do NOT paint water drops or fog on the glass; interactive canvas will add them. No text, no UI, no people, no logos. Keep exquisite photography and atmospheric depth. This is the portrait adaptation of the same scene, not a new location.
