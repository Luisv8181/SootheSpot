# SootheSpot Digital Bookshelf

SootheSpot's Resource Explorer is a curated external-resource index presented as a digital bookshelf.

The purpose is navigation, not content ownership. SootheSpot stores metadata and provenance and normally sends people to the original resource.

## Shelves

**Read** - books, articles, essays, fact sheets and psychoeducation.

**Listen** - guided meditations, breathing practices and calming audio.

**Practice** - grounding, relaxation, mindfulness, journaling, sensory and movement practices.

**Watch** - educational videos and guided demonstrations.

**Sleep & Rest** - sleep tools, body scans, wind-down practices and sleep education.

**Understand Yourself** - approachable material about stress, anxiety, mood, grief, relationships, trauma and related topics.

**Reach Out** - professional, community and safety/support pathways.

## Discovery

Browse shelves or start from a need: calm down, sleep, read, listen, practice, understand, or find support.

## Trust

Prefer primary sources. Preserve attribution, review status, limitations and last verification. Do not copy third-party content or create opaque universal scores.

## Books

Books are reference records only. Store bibliographic metadata and legitimate publisher, library or bookstore links. Do not store copied book text.

## Safety

Safety and crisis resources remain a separate pathway. Ordinary coping browsing should not silently become crisis routing.

See the dependency-ordered resource library roadmap maintained in this document's roadmap section and current pull request notes.
