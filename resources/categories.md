# Resource Categories

SootheSpot is both a curated resource index and a calm digital bookshelf. Records link to original publishers; third-party content is not copied into the repository.

## Shelves

- **Read**: books, articles, essays, fact sheets, psychoeducation
- **Listen**: guided meditations, breathing practices and calming audio
- **Practice**: grounding, breathing, mindfulness, relaxation, journaling, sensory and movement practices
- **Watch**: educational videos and guided demonstrations
- **Sleep & Rest**: sleep education, wind-down audio, body scans and relaxation
- **Understand Yourself**: stress, anxiety, mood, grief, relationships, trauma and related psychoeducation
- **Reach Out**: finding help, support groups, professional care and safety/support pathways

## Discovery needs

calm down, sleep, read, listen, understand, practice, find support.

## Metadata

Need, duration, format, language, locale/region, accessibility, independent-use, therapist-supported, evidence/provenance, privacy/data practices, review status, last verification.

Tags are descriptive metadata, not clinical recommendations.
